Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-30.

life-kb is used by exactly one person: its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account, after the owner grants permission on Google's consent screen.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages and adds its own labels (kb/personal, kb/commercial, kb/promotional). It does not send, delete, or archive mail.
  • Google Drive: reads files and writes to a folder it created.

Where data goes

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.

life-kb's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
